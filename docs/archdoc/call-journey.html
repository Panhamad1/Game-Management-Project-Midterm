Edge Cases, Safeguards & Combat Balance Rules

Edge Case / State Potential Failure Mode Implemented Architecture Solution
Prop Screen Obstruction Carried large sandstone boulders or slabs block forward vision and crosshair. Bottom-Right Carry Pocket: Props snap to offset (0.42m, -0.32m, 1.35m) relative to the camera, guaranteeing the crosshair remains 100% unobstructed.
Infinite Wall Climbing Player holds Space while looking at a wall to climb infinitely into the sky. State-Gated Wall Cling: Wall-hang lasts a strict 1.5 seconds with downward slide (0.35 m/s). Vaulting (+9.5m) consumes the hang state and cannot re-latch without touching ground or entering a new wall run.
Fast Projectile Tunneling During Parry High-speed sniper bolts pass through the player between frames before Push can register. Hemispherical Overlap Sweep: TryDeflect() sweeps all layers within 3.8m radius plus a 6.5m forward cone, detecting fast bolts before they reach player collision.
Swarmer Block Crush Fling Swarmers stay stationary after being crushed by a thrown 45kg obsidian slab. Physical Impulse Displacement: OnPhysicsCrush flings the Swarmer rigidbody along the impact vector at high velocity (linearVelocity = knockbackDir * (speed * 1.6f) + Vector3.up * 4.2f).
Hulk Ground Smash Screen Shake Distance Hulk smashes the floor 100 meters away and the player screen shakes violently. 20m Distance Attenuation: Camera shake only fires if Vector3.Distance(playerPos, smashPos) < 20f, scaling down cleanly to 0 at the 20m perimeter.
Slow Motion Visual Clarity Full-screen grey filters make dark chambers impossible to navigate. 12px Edge Accent Framing: Slow motion keeps center viewport 100% crisp and uncolored, framed only by subtle electric cyan/gold border accents and time scale indicators.
Un-Crouch Under Low Obstacles Player releases Crouch inside a low stone gate, clipping head into geometry. Overhead Clearance Raycast: Checks Physics.Raycast(head, Vector3.up, 1.0m). If blocked, crouch height remains locked at 0.9m until fully clear.
