Edge Cases & Physics Safeguards

Edge Case Potential Problem Our Implemented Solution
Box-Surfing Glitch Standing on a crate and pulling it causes infinite flying into space. Grounded Check Lockout: If PlayerMovement.GroundedObject == target, pulling is blocked completely.
Snagging Behind Walls Walking around a pillar while pulling traps the box or clips it into walls. Leash Break: If line of sight is broken by geometry or distance exceeds 15m, the tether snaps and drops the block.
Fast Block Collision A fast-flying box could crush or clip inside the player. Soft Pushback: Blocks gently slide off the capsule without crushing or causing self-damage.
Heavy Blocks Lifting giant 25kg blocks breaks puzzle balance. Ground Drag Only: Heavy blocks drag across the floor; they cannot be lifted into the air.
Crate Cannon Combo Pressing LMB while holding RMB. Instant Launch: Disengages pull lock and fires the block forward as a high-speed missile.
