Edge Cases, Safeguards & Combat Balance Rules

Edge Case / State Potential Failure Mode Implemented Architecture Solution
Spider-Sense Threat Stacking Multiple snipers or Hulk slams happen at the same time, confusing HUD chevrons. Priority & Multi-Arc Clamping: The closest immediate lethal attack flashes with highest opacity; secondary threats display smaller faint chevrons.
Sniper Reflection Alignment Player Push deflection fires in a random direction and misses the sniper. Laser-Trace Reversal: The deflected bullet calculates its return trajectory along the sniper's own targeting ray, guaranteeing a direct hit if deflected cleanly.
Hulk Ground Slam Jitter Player is near the slam edge and gets stuck in physics jitter between capsule and wave. Trigger Volume Sphere-Cast: Shockwave is evaluated as an expanding radial trigger volume with clear 1.5m height check (jump/grapple clears).
Swarmer Ramming at Low Speed Player walks into a Swarmer pack at 3 m/s expecting a bowling strike. Speed Threshold (12 m/s): Bowling strike only triggers at slide/grapple speed (>=12 m/s). Low-speed collisions deal contact damage to player.
Carrying Props in Tight Corridors A crate held 2.5m ahead jams into doorframes or wedges player against corners. Elastic Retraction: Proximity sensors retract the hover anchor from 2.5m to 1.2m when walls are detected, smoothing narrow corridor navigation.
Wall Collision at 35 m/s Player or thrown crate tunnels through thin walls or floor meshes. ContinuousDynamic CCD: Rigidbodies evaluate sweep volumes every sub-step; static level walls are modeled with minimum 0.5m thickness.
Un-Crouch Under Low Obstacles Player releases Crouch inside a low vent, clipping head into geometry. Overhead Clearance Raycast: Checks Physics.Raycast(head, Vector3.up, 1.0m). If blocked, crouch height remains locked at 0.9m until fully clear.
Target Ambiguity (Wall vs Crate) Player wants to grapple a wall behind an object, or grab a crate in front of a wall. Target-Priority Hierarchy: Layer 10 (Interactables) and Layer 12 (Light Enemies) take precedence if directly under the crosshair; otherwise falls back to Layer 9/14 (Grapple).
