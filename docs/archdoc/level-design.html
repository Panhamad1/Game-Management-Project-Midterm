Level Architecture · Egyptian Academy

6-Chamber Egyptian Parkour & Combat Academy

A comprehensive chamber-by-chamber blueprint for REBOUND's 180-meter linear Egyptian tutorial. Each chamber systematically teaches one core parkour or kinetic combat skill with physical checkpoints and instant respawns.

Level Design Core Principles

The Egyptian Academy is structured as a single continuous 180-meter linear facility built from monumental sandstone, polished brass, obsidian, and lapis lazuli. The environment itself teaches every mechanic through physical obstacles rather than intrusive popups:

Core Principles

  • Immediate Objective: Each chamber presents a clear visual goal at its entrance (a slide gate, an elevated ledge, or an Ankh target).
  • One Lesson Per Chamber: Teaches a single mechanic (e.g. wall-hang climb vs bullet deflection) before combining skills in the final sanctum.
  • Checkpoint Teleportation: Stepping through a chamber archway records a checkpoint. Falling into the void instantly respawns the player at the start of that chamber.
  • Readability Across Distance: Throw-able props project constant ambient glows (Mint Jade, Amber Gold, Molten Ruby) so their mass and function are readable from across the room.

Banned Level Anti-Patterns

  • No Invisible Walls: Boundaries and pits are physically walled by massive sandstone pylons.
  • No Screen-Obstructing Props: Pulled objects rest in a bottom-right pocket, keeping the center crosshair clear.
  • No Unfair Enemy Hitscan: All enemy attacks feature distinct visual telegraphs (laser sightlines or leap wind-ups).
  • No Irreversible Softlocks: Target switches and void planes ensure failure results in an instant 0.1s reset rather than game restarts.

Chamber # Distance Core Lesson Physical Layout & Obstacle Verified Solution & Keybinds
Chamber 1 0m - 30m Locomotion & Slide Open sandstone corridor leading to a 1.2m low stone gate blocking the exit archway. Hold Left Shift to sprint at 5.4 m/s, press Ctrl to slide at 4.8 m/s under the gate.
Chamber 2 30m - 60m Wall-Run & Wall-Jump A wide bottomless chasm with alternating vertical sandstone panels on left and right walls. Sprint and jump onto a wall; hold forward to wall-run, then tap Space + A/D to zig-zag jump across.
Chamber 3 60m - 90m Wall-Hang & Climb Vault A 7m tall sheer sandstone cliff face with an elevated upper terrace. Too high for a regular jump. Leap at the wall to latch into a 1.5s wall-hang, then tap Space to execute a +9.5m vertical climb vault onto the terrace.
Chamber 4 90m - 120m Kinetic Prop Weaponry A locked gate guarded by an elevated Ankh target switch. Three glowing props sit nearby (Urn, Boulder, Slab). Hold RMB to tractor a prop into your bottom-right pocket, aim at the Ankh target, and tap LMB to launch at 32 m/s.
Chamber 5 120m - 150m Chrono-Dilation & Deflection A defensive corridor guarded by an automated plasma Sentry turret firing continuous bolts. Tap Z to dilate time (25% speed), aim at incoming plasma bolts, and tap LMB within 3.8m to deflect them back (+20 Focus).
Chamber 6 150m - 185m Combat Mastery Sanctum A grand arena housing Swarmers, a high-leaping Hulk, an elevated Sniper, and a Sentry turret. Combine all skills: throw heavy props to stagger Hulk and bowl Swarmers, deflect sniper bolts, and slide under attacks to finish the trial.

Void Kill Plane & Instant Checkpoint Reset

A continuous trigger collider spans the entire 180m facility at y = -8.0m (named Void_Kill_Plane). Stepping or falling through this plane instantly relocates the player's Rigidbody to the spawn anchor of their most recent chamber checkpoint in 0.05 seconds, preserving gameplay momentum with zero loading screens.
