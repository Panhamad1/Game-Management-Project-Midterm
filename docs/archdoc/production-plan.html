Every subsystem has exactly one primary owner. This ensures clear accountability and prevents team members from overwriting each other's code:

Person 1 Player & Camera Lead First-person WASD movement, mouse look pitch/yaw, jump physics, ground check, Rigidbody constraints, and responding to recoil impulses from the push ability. A responsive Rigidbody player controller that moves, jumps, and recoils reliably without tipping over.
Person 2 Abilities & Physics Objects Target detection raycasting, right-hand push impulse, left-hand continuous pull force, AbilityTarget component, mass tuning, and dual-hand target lock. A stable push and pull mechanic that manipulates test crates smoothly without wall clipping.

3. Technical Risk Mitigation Matrix

Identified risks from OPEN_QUESTIONS_AND_RISKS.md and PHYSICS_AND_UNITY_NOTES.md paired with concrete preventative solutions:

Risk ID Severity Failure Mode Description Empirical Engineering Solution
RISK-01 Critical Slow motion also slows player movement: Reducing Time.timeScale causes the player Rigidbody to move at 25% speed, defeating the entire gameplay purpose. Solution: When slow motion triggers, multiply player movement force and jump impulse by (1.0f / Time.timeScale) to compensate, and calculate camera look using Time.unscaledDeltaTime. If global time remains unstable, switch to per-object time control on puzzle entities.
RISK-02 Critical Objects clipping through walls when pulled: High pull acceleration causes crates to tunnel through thin wall colliders. Solution: Enable CollisionDetectionMode.Continuous on all interactable crates. Enforce an 8.0 m/s maximum velocity clamp in PullAbility.FixedUpdate() and use thick (0.5m+) wall colliders.
RISK-03 High Both hands grabbing the same object: Left hand pulls while right hand pushes, causing opposing impulses that crash or violently vibrate the object. Solution: Enforce a target lock in AbilityTarget.cs: if IsControlled == true, reject interactions from the other hand until released.
RISK-04 High Player recoil tipping capsule over: Powerful upward or downward push recoil causes the Rigidbody capsule to fall over like a bowling pin. Solution: Check Freeze Rotation X and Freeze Rotation Z in the Rigidbody inspector. Rotate the camera pivot independently for pitch.
RISK-05 Medium Git merge conflicts in scene files: Two team members work on the same scene simultaneously, corrupting Unity's YAML serialization. Solution: Strict rule: only one person edits a given .unity scene file at a time. All reusable assets, triggers, and level blocks must be built as Prefabs.
RISK-06 Medium Irreversible puzzle softlocks: An essential crate falls off a ledge into an unreachable pit, making the room impossible to complete. Solution: Attach an auto-respawner trigger in every hazard pit that resets dropped puzzle objects back to their initial spawn transform.
