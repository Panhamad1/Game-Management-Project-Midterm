# REBOUND Enemy Gameplay Guide

This guide describes the four enemy types in REBOUND, their attack patterns, and how to defeat them.

---

## Player Controls in Combat

| Input | Action | What It Does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Left Click (LMB)** | **Push** | Blasts objects and enemies within 12m. When an enemy bullet approaches, this **deflects the bullet** back at enemies with double speed and damage. |
| **Hold Right Click (RMB)** | **Pull** | Pulls props or Swarmers into your bottom-right pocket. Bullets and floors cannot be pulled. |
| **LMB while holding prop** | **Crate Cannon** | Fires the carried object at `32 m/s` as a kinetic projectile. |
| **Z** | **Slow Motion (Focus)** | Slows the world to 25% speed so you have plenty of time to dodge, aim, or deflect enemy bullets. |
| **Left Shift + Ctrl** | **Power Slide** | Slide at `4.8 m/s` with 360-degree carving. High-speed slides instantly crush Swarmers. |
| **Space** | **Jump & Parkour** | Jumps over shockwaves. When wall-hanging, press Space to vault `+9.5m` up over cliffs. When wall-running, press Space to zig-zag jump across gaps. |

## 1. Small Fast Swarmer

**Health**: 20 HP.

A flying insect-like drone that hovers and lunges at you.

### How It Attacks
1. Spots you within **25 meters** and flies toward you at up to **10 m/s**.
2. Within **5 meters**, it turns **bright red** for a **0.5-second** warning telegraph.
3. Lunges forward at **11 m/s**. If it hits you, it deals **15 damage**.

### How to Defeat It
- **Slide into it**: Sliding at `4.8 m/s` or faster crushes it instantly.
- **Push**: Knocks it back and deals `10 damage`. Two hits defeat it.
- **Throw a Block at it**: Striking a Swarmer with a thrown boulder or urn deals heavy damage and sends it **flying backward through the air** like a ragdoll.
- **Grab and Throw**: Pull it with `RMB` and throw it with `LMB`. A thrown Swarmer has a **65% chance** to explode on impact into a high-yield plasma detonation (`6.5m` blast radius, 75 damage), wiping out nearby enemies!

## 2. Rapid-Laser Sentry

**Health**: 50 HP.

A stationary turret that locks onto you and fires rapid laser bursts.

### How It Attacks
1. Spots you within **30 meters** and begins firing.
2. Fires a burst of **3 red laser bolts** (0.25s apart) traveling at **22 m/s**. Each bolt deals **5 damage**.
3. Pauses for **1.5 seconds** between bursts.

### How to Defeat It
- **Deflect its bolts**: Stand in the line of fire and tap `LMB` (Push) as the bolts arrive. The bolts deflect back at the Sentry at **35 m/s**, flashing cyan and dealing double damage.
- **Block with an Obsidian Slab**: Hold an Obsidian Slab with `RMB` as a riot shield. It absorbs all incoming laser bolts.
- **Throw a Boulder at it**: Launching a sandstone boulder deals up to **45 damage** and knocks the turret backward. Follow up with another hit to defeat a full-health turret.

---

## 3. Hulk Leaper

**Health**: 90 HP.

A massive armored brute with devastating melee swings and a leaping ground slam.

### How It Attacks
- **Melee Swing**: If you get within **2.3 meters**, he hits for **20 damage** and knocks you back.
- **Leap Warning**: Within **12 meters**, he flashes **orange** for **0.8 seconds** before launching into the air.
- **Ground Slam Shockwave**: On landing, an orange shockwave ring expands outward across the ground up to **14 meters** dealing **30 damage**.
- **Proximity Screen Shake**: When the Hulk slams the ground, your camera shakes heavily if you are within **20 meters**.

### How to Defeat It
- **Jump the Shockwave**: When he lands, tap `Space` to jump over the expanding orange ring.
- **Throw Boulders at Armor**: Throwing sandstone boulders or urns at his armor deals damage, flashes his model bright red, spawns floating damage numbers, and **physically staggers him backward**.
- **Hit the Exposed Core**: After landing his leap, he is stunned for **2.5 seconds** with his vulnerable back core exposed. Hitting his core deals massive critical crush damage.

---

## 4. Sniper Enemy

**Health**: 10 HP.

Stationed on high perches at long range.

### How It Attacks
1. Locks onto you with a bright red laser beam for **1.5 seconds**.
2. Fires a high-velocity sonic shot that deals lethal damage.

### How to Defeat It
- Activate Slow Motion with `Z`.
- Aim your crosshair directly at the Sniper.
- Click `LMB` (Push) the moment the red shot fires to deflect it straight back into the Sniper for an instant kill.
